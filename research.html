Research Programme

Rigorous control for systems that learn and operate under uncertainty.

My research bridges mathematical control theory and modern data-driven intelligence—developing controllers with provable stability, delay robustness, and safety guarantees for autonomous robots, smart energy infrastructure, and biomedical systems. At Glasgow Caledonian University, I founder-lead the Robotics for the Common Good research group.
